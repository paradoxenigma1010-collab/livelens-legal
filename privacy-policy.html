Privacy Policy for LiveLens

Effective Date: 2026-08-09
Last Updated: 2026-08-09

LiveLens ("the App") is a general-purpose IPTV and media streaming client. This privacy policy explains what data the App collects, how it's used, and your rights.

Data Collection

LiveLens stores the following data locally on your device only (not transmitted to any server):

  • Playlist URLs and credentials: M3U playlist URLs, Xtream Codes account credentials (host, username, password), and EPG (electronic program guide) URLs you add to the app. These are stored in your device's local storage (Android SharedPreferences) to remember your sources between app sessions.
  • Saved streams: Names and URLs of individual streams you save to your favorites list.
  • App preferences: UI settings like category order, tour completion flags, and filter preferences.

No data leaves your device or is sent to the app developer. LiveLens does not have analytics, tracking, crash reporting, or any network communication except:

  1. Fetching your playlists/streams: When you add an IPTV source, the app fetches the M3U playlist or EPG data directly from the URL or server you provide. This is a direct connection between your device and that source — the app developer is not involved and does not see this traffic.
  2. Public playlist discovery (optional feature): The "Discover playlists" feature fetches a list of public M3U sources from the iptv-org project on GitHub. No personal data is sent; this is a simple HTTP request to a public repository.
  3. Stream playback: When you play a channel, your device connects directly to the stream URL from your playlist. The app developer does not host, proxy, or monitor this traffic.

Third-Party Data

The app connects to sources you provide (IPTV services, M3U playlists, Xtream Codes panels). Those services have their own privacy policies and may log your IP address, viewing habits, or other data. LiveLens has no control over or visibility into third-party data practices. You are responsible for reviewing the privacy policies of any IPTV service or playlist source you use.

Data Security

All data is stored locally on your device using Android's standard secure storage (SharedPreferences). Xtream Codes passwords are stored in plaintext locally (same trust level as your WiFi passwords or other app credentials stored by Android). The app does not encrypt this data at rest, and anyone with device administrator access or physical access to your unlocked device could read it. Do not use this app on a shared or untrusted device if you are concerned about credential security.

Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time:

  • Remove individual playlists or streams within the app
  • Clear all app data via Android Settings → Apps → LiveLens → Storage → Clear Data
  • Uninstall the app to remove all stored data

Children's Privacy

LiveLens is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has used this app, contact us at [Your Email] and we will delete any stored data.

Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device and nothing is sent to the developer, your rights (access, correction, deletion) are exercised by managing the app's local storage directly on your device as described above.
